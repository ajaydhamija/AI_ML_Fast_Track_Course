What changed in this edition

  • Four new lectures (6–9): neural networks (hidden layers and backpropagation), convolutional networks, Transformers intuitively, and Transformers technically. Each has slides, a reference text, an instructor manual, a 15-question quiz, an assignment with full solutions, and an interactive studio.
  • Renumbered: the reinforcement-learning lectures are now 10–12, revision is 13 and projects are 14. All cross-references, manuals and studios carry the new numbers; the studio links for Lectures 10–13 are unchanged.
  • Lecture 13 (revision) adds the deep-learning block to the concept map, method comparison, formula compendium, practice questions and air-defence case, a bonus deep-learning problem, and four new tools in the Revision Studio.
  • Lecture 14 (projects) adds CNN and Transformer project themes, a reference-text section on deep-learning projects, a quiz question on honest splits and a bonus project-planning problem.
