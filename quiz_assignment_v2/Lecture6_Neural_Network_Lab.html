DEMO 2A hidden layer re-maps the data

The lecture's network: h₁ = f(x₁ + x₂), h₂ = f(x₁ + x₂ + c₂), ŷ = h₁ − 2h₂. With ReLU and c₂ = −1 it computes XOR exactly. Switch the activation to the identity and the two layers collapse into one straight line. The right panel shows where each input lands in hidden space.

DEMO 4Why deep sigmoid networks stop learning

Each layer the error passes back through multiplies it by a weight and by the activation's slope f′(z). The sigmoid's slope is at most 0.25, so the product shrinks geometrically with depth. ReLU's slope is exactly 1 on active units.
